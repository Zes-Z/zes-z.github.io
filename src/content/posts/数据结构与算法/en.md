## Keywords

* `Catalan numbers`
> General term formula (closed form):  $$C_n = \frac{1}{n+1} \binom{2n}{n} = \frac{(2n)!}{(n+1)!n!}$$
>
> Recurrence formula (commonly used in dynamic programming):  $$C_0 = 1，C_n = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} C_i \times C_{n-1-i} \quad (n \ge 1)$$
>
> Linear recurrence formula (most efficient):$$C_0 = 1，C_n = \frac{2(2n-1)}{n+1} C_{n-1}$$
